USER GUIDE // KOSERA V8
Craft smarter, together
This guide covers everything you need to use the Fabrication Kiosk as an org member — placing fabrication orders, tracking their progress through Fulfillment, and managing your personal ore stock and unlocked blueprints.
CONTENTS
01
Getting Started
🚀
The kiosk runs in your browser — no install needed. Open the link your org administrator shared, wait a moment for it to sync with the org database, and you're in.
→
What you see on load
The kiosk opens on the Orders screen — a blueprint picker on the left and your order builder on the right. The title bar shows the connection status: a green dot and CONNECTED means you're synced to the org database.
→
Navigation
The left sidebar has three icons: Orders (clipboard) — place new fabrication requests, Fulfillment (checkmark) — track every order's status, and Inventory (person) — manage your ore stock and unlocked blueprints. Click any icon to switch screens.
→
No account yet?
Ask your org admin to add you under the Inventory screen. Once added you'll appear in all user dropdowns across the kiosk. You can browse and place orders as a guest without logging in, but tracking your own blueprint unlocks and ore stock requires selecting your profile.
02
Logging In
🔑
You don't need to log in to browse blueprints or view the ore manifest. But logging in links actions to your profile — your blueprints, your ores, and your orders.
→
Using the LOGIN button
Click LOGIN in the title bar (right side). A prompt appears — select your name from the list. If your account has a password, enter it when asked. Your callsign appears in the title bar once logged in.
→
Selecting your user in a dropdown
Any dropdown that says Select user works the same way — pick your name, enter your password if prompted. Your selection carries across the current session.
→
Changing your password
Go to the Inventory screen and select your profile. A CHANGE PASSWORD button is visible when your own account is loaded. Enter a new password and confirm. Your admin can also reset it directly in the sheet if needed.
PASSWORDS ARE OPTIONAL If your account has no password set, selecting your name immediately loads your profile — no prompt appears.
03
Placing an Order
📦
The Orders screen is a blueprint picker on the left and your order builder on the right — everything happens on this one screen now. Once you place an order, your org's artificers see it in the Fulfillment queue and craft it for you.
FILTERING THE BLUEPRINT LIST
Click a category icon to show only that item type. Hover a category icon for a sub-category dropdown. Toggle My BP's to see only blueprints you've unlocked. Use the search bar to find something by name.
WHOSE BLUEPRINTS AM I SEEING?
The dropdown above the search bar controls this: All Blueprints shows the entire catalog, picking a specific member shows only what they've unlocked, and the default — ⚙ Gnome Admin Master List — shows every blueprint any member of the org has unlocked, combined.
01
Select your blueprints
Click a blueprint row to check it — click again to uncheck. You can select as many as you want; a counter badge appears on the + ADD TO ORDER button showing how many are selected.
02
Set quantities
Click + ADD TO ORDER to open a quantity panel listing every selected blueprint. Use − / + per item to set how many you need, or ✕ to drop one from the selection. Click ADD ALL TO ORDER when ready.
03
Review Order Items & Total Materials
Added items appear in the ORDER ITEMS list on the right, with running quantities you can still adjust or remove. The TOTAL MATERIALS panel beside it shows the combined ore cost across every item in the order.
04
Enter Callsign and RSI Handle
Both fields are required — they tell your artificer exactly who to deliver the finished items to in-game. If you're logged in, your account name is recorded on the order automatically; if not, it's logged as Guest.
05
Add a note (optional)
Click ADD NOTE for special instructions — preferred ore quality, urgency, delivery location, or anything your artificer should know before starting.
06
Place the order
Click PLACE ORDER. It saves instantly to the org sheet and shows up in Fulfillment with a New Order status.
04
Tracking Orders
🔍
The Fulfillment screen (checkmark icon) lists every order the org has placed — including yours — so you can watch its progress without asking anyone.
→
Finding an order
Use the ORDERS / COMPLETE toggle to switch between active and finished orders. Use the Status and Assigned dropdowns to narrow the list — each lets you check/uncheck multiple values at once. Click ▼ ALL / ▲ ALL to expand or collapse every card.
→
Reading an order card
Click a card to expand it and see every item in the order, the combined materials needed, and any notes. As your artificer finishes each item they check it off, so an expanded card shows exactly what's done and what's still pending.

05
Your Inventory
🧰
The Inventory screen (person icon) is where you log your personal ore stock and mark which blueprints you've unlocked. Select your name in the USER dropdown (password required if your account has one) to load and edit your own profile.
EDITING VS. VIEWING
You can only ever add, change, or remove ore entries and blueprint unlocks on your own logged-in profile. To look at someone else's stock without touching it, use the separate VIEW dropdown — anyone can browse anyone's inventory read-only this way, no password needed.
WHY KEEP IT CURRENT
Your ore log and unlocked blueprints are what your officers and artificers see when planning what the org can actually build and who to assign it to. An out-of-date log means someone might get sent to fetch materials you already have.
→
Adding an ore entry
In the Materials panel, click an ore from the type filter row. A modal opens — enter the quality (0–1000) and quantity (SCU for most ores, units/rocks for hand-mined ones like Dolivine or Hadanite), then click COMPLETE. You can log multiple stacks of the same ore at different quality levels.
→
Editing or deleting an entry
Hover any material entry in your list to reveal the edit (pencil) and delete (✕) buttons. Edit reopens the same modal pre-filled with that stack's current quality and quantity.
→
My Ores filter
Toggle My Ores to switch between browsing every ore type (to add something new) and viewing only what you already have in stock.
→
Marking blueprints as owned
The left panel lists every blueprint. With your profile loaded, click a row to toggle it between owned (teal highlight + checkmark) and not owned — this saves to your profile automatically. Toggle My BP's to filter the list down to just what you own.

06
Tips & Quick Reference
⚡
A few things worth knowing that will save you time.
→
Not sure who has a blueprint unlocked?
Leave the Orders screen's dropdown on ⚙ Gnome Admin Master List (the default) — it shows every blueprint unlocked by anyone in the org, so you're never stuck browsing just your own list.
→
Add notes for special requests
If you'd like your order crafted from higher-quality ore, or have a delivery preference, say so in the order's ADD NOTE field — artificers see it right on the expanded order card in Fulfillment.
→
Keep your ore log current
Update your Inventory entries after every mining run. It's the only source the kiosk has for what you actually own — nothing is inferred automatically.
→
Check on a teammate before asking
Use the VIEW dropdown on the Inventory screen to see what ore or blueprints a teammate has logged before pinging them directly — it's read-only, so there's no risk of changing their data by mistake.
→
The kiosk syncs live
All data (orders, ores, blueprint unlocks) lives in the org Google Sheet and syncs every 30 seconds. If a teammate just placed an order or updated their ore log, you'll see it within half a minute without refreshing.
QUESTIONS? If something looks wrong — the connection dot shows NOT CONNECTED, an order is stuck, or your account won't load — contact your org admin. They have access to the backend sheet and can fix most issues directly.
